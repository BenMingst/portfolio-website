[ About This Site ]

This portfolio is intentionally designed to feel like an Autodesk Inventor environment: a CAD-style window with ribbons, a browser tree, technical sheets, and title blocks.

Design choices

  • CAD-style sheets, rulers, and title blocks to present projects like engineering drawings.
  • Ribbon tabs and a browser tree inspired by Inventor’s interface.
  • Layout tuned to feel familiar to anyone who has used professional 3D CAD tools.

Why it matters

  • Connects my 9+ years of Inventor and 3D modeling experience to web development.
  • Shows how I think about UX for technical and engineering audiences.
  • Sets the stage for interactive 3D content in the 3D Viewer section.

How to navigate this portfolio

This site is designed like a CAD workspace. Here are a few tips:

  • Top ribbon tabs open different pages like Experience, Projects, Skills, and more.
  • Left “Page” panel lets you jump to sections such as Overview and Contact.
  • Scroll through the technical sheets to read the content.
  • Toggle fullscreen with the button in the top-right title bar.
  • Use the 3D Viewer tab to explore interactive 3D content.
